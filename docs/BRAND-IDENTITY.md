# Royal City identity

## Idea

Royal City is a residential ecosystem for people, homes, communities and useful local services. Its identity should feel calm, precise and human. Visual polish must not imply capabilities or outcomes the product cannot verify.

## Official source artwork

- `web/royal-city-logo.png` is the unchanged official logo artwork from the Royal City source project (`public/brand/royal-city-logo-green.png`). It contains the original deep-green crown and stacked Royal City wordmark.
- `web/royal-city-emblem.png` and the iOS `RoyalCityEmblem` image set are transparent crops of that crown, made only for compact placements. The web header pairs this exact crop with the Royal City name.
- Keep the source artwork intact. Do not redraw or recolor the crown; use the light backing tile on dark interface surfaces so the official green remains legible.
- Supplied JPEGs remain source artwork. IMG_1510 is BeatHealth art; IMG_1505 is a retired predecessor-brand concept and is not the Royal City logo.

## Visual language

- Deep ink blue establishes trust and gives the interface a composed architectural field.
- Mineral teal signals connection and living systems; champagne marks considered emphasis and craft.
- Warm white keeps typography legible and less clinical.
- The original crown is the sole Royal City emblem. Navigation uses a consistent set of restrained line icons. Cards use clear hierarchy, purposeful spacing and explicit status, avoiding decorative dashboard clutter.

## Product principles

Keep Royal City communities, homes and resident services visible in the landing hierarchy. Present intelligence as a governed capability within the ecosystem, not as the product itself. Distinguish proposals from authorization and verified outcomes. Use real evidence for claims, occupancy, service availability and activity.

The Royal City logo comes from its source project. References to NASA or SpaceX inform clarity and mission-led hierarchy only; their marks are not used.
