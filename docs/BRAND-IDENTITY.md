# Zalagren identity

## Idea

Zalagren connects people, places and services through clear context and accountable outcomes. Its identity should feel calm, precise and human. It is a platform for real communities, so visual polish must not imply capabilities or outcomes the product cannot verify.

## Master assets

- `web/zalagren-wordmark.svg` is the horizontal master lockup for the global platform header.
- `web/zalagren-emblem.svg` is the standalone symbol for the menu, favicon and installable app icon.
- Both are editable vector sources. Keep their proportions and contrast; do not add effects or recolor individual uses outside the palette.
- Supplied JPEGs remain source artwork. IMG_1510 is BeatHealth art; IMG_1505 is a retired Zalagren concept and is not the platform logo.

## Visual language

- Deep ink blue establishes trust and gives the interface a composed architectural field.
- Mineral teal signals connection and living systems; champagne marks considered emphasis and craft.
- Warm white keeps typography legible and less clinical.
- Thin routes and connected nodes in the emblem express movement through people, place, capability and evidence. The open orbit suggests a living system rather than a closed institution.
- Navigation uses a consistent set of restrained line icons. Cards use clear hierarchy, purposeful spacing and explicit status, avoiding decorative dashboard clutter.

## Product principles

Keep community and residential services visible in the landing hierarchy. Present intelligence as a governed capability within the platform, not as the platform itself. Distinguish proposals from authorization and verified outcomes. Use real evidence for claims, occupancy, service availability and activity.

The mark is an original Zalagren vector direction; it does not reproduce NASA, SpaceX or another organization's insignia. Those references inform clarity and mission-led hierarchy only.
