# Zalagren Services Contract

## Purpose

Service is the canonical operational definition of something a Provider can offer to a Participant or Community.

## Invariants

1. Every Service has a Provider.
2. Provider identity is separate from Service.
3. Capability is not authorization.
4. Subscription entitlement is not authorization.
5. Eligibility is not authorization.
6. A Service Request does not authorize protected execution.
7. Authorization is evaluated by Core authorization/governance.
8. Service completion requires an observable event/evidence path.
9. A failed, cancelled or disputed service must not be represented as successful.
10. Service data must be minimized to the stated purpose.
11. Pricing, tax, qualifications, safety, legal and regulatory claims require their own evidence.
12. Offline/pending states must remain explicit.

## Lifecycle

Need → Intent → Request → Eligibility → Proposal → Authorization → Scheduling → Action → Event → Evidence → Resolution → Learning.

## Safety

Zalagren may coordinate services and providers, but it must not claim that a provider is licensed, insured, qualified, vetted or legally authorized unless authoritative evidence has been verified and recorded.

Kenyan data-protection obligations must be handled according to the applicable legal basis, purpose, safeguards and registration requirements.
