# Organizations

Organizations are first-class entities representing companies, institutions, cooperatives,
nonprofits, government entities, community organizations and other structured entities.

An Organization is not a human Participant and does not replace Participant identity.
A person may represent, work for, own, manage, or provide services through an organization
using Relationships, Contexts, Capabilities and Authorization.

An organization may operate communities, places and provider profiles without receiving
automatic access to the global data of the people connected to it.

Closing an organization preserves its identity and historical evidence.
