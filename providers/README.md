# Providers

Providers represent entities capable of delivering a service, resource, work or other
legitimate capability through Zalagren.

A provider may be backed by:
- an individual Participant;
- an Organization;
- a community or institution.

Provider status is independent from authorization. Being an active provider does not
automatically authorize access to a place, participant data, funds or protected operations.

Capability, service area and verification references are descriptive/provider-readiness
data; operational permission remains contextual and authorization-controlled.

A provider can operate across multiple places and contexts without creating duplicate accounts.
