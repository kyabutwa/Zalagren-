# Zalagren Beat Services

These services are product/service domains inside the single Zalagren ecosystem. They are not separate identity systems or separate Zalagren applications.

## BeatPay
Payments coordination through supported regulated rails such as M-PESA and bank integrations. Safaricom documents M-PESA APIs including C2B, B2C, B2B, Dynamic QR and M-PESA Express; actual production integration requires credentials, contracts and verification. citeturn0search13turn0search14

## BeatFood
Food discovery, ordering and fulfilment coordination.

## BeatHealth
Health navigation and coordination: providers, appointments, pharmacies, care workflows and related opportunities. Health information is sensitive; Kenya's Digital Health Act and associated regulatory framework require strong safeguards around digital-health and health information workflows. citeturn0search37turn0search40

## BeatGenzi
Opportunity and economic coordination: jobs, services, providers, partnerships, training and practical opportunities.

## BeatMarket
Legitimate marketplace coordination for goods and services, with category restrictions, seller/provider evidence and transaction records.

## BeatRide
Mobility coordination between participants and participating transport providers. It does not make Zalagren the transport operator.

## BeatBnB
Accommodation coordination for hosts, guests, places, availability, booking and stay evidence.

## Shared Zalagren rule

All seven use the same core:
Identity → Account → Participant → Relationship → Context → Capability → Authorization → Intent → Proposal → Action → Event → Evidence.

They do not create separate accounts per service.

A service can be offered directly to a person, through a community, through an organization, or through a provider relationship, depending on authorization and context.

## Truth boundary

Adding these definitions does not mean live providers, payment rails, hospitals, restaurants, drivers, sellers, hosts, prices or availability have been connected. Those must be independently onboarded and verified.
