# Communities

Community is a first-class Zalagren domain.

A community may represent a residential community, apartment environment, estate, building, small city, campus, institution, or another governed participating environment.

Communities do not own a person's global Zalagren identity.

Canonical boundary:

Identity -> Account -> Participant -> Relationship -> Context -> Community

People may enter Zalagren without a community. Communities may later establish relationships with those canonical participants.

Community management will later compose people, organizations, places, real estate, providers, services, resources, utilities, requests, work, governance, events, and evidence.

Membership, roles, and authorization are deliberately not embedded in the Community record.
